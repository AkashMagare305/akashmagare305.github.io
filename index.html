Pre-Treatment & Cathodic Electrodeposition (PTCD)

Automotive aur Industrial surface coating ka foundation: Corrosion Resistance aur Primer Base Adhesion System.

01

Degreasing

Metal surface se oil, grease, shop dirt aur organic impurities ko alkaline chemicals se clean kiya jata hai.

02

Water Rinse & Activation

Chemical residues ko remove karke titanium activator solution se uniform phosphating crystal nucleation tayar kiya jata hai.

03

Zinc Phosphating

Metal par microscopic Zinc Phosphate crystal layer banti hai jo rust protection aur CED primer adhesion provide karti hai.

04

CED Bath & Baking

Cathodic Electrodeposition me DC voltage se 15-20 micron uniform primer layer deposit hoke 180°C oven me cure hoti hai.

PTCD Critical Quality Parameters

Bath Conductivity & pH

CED Bath pH (5.8 - 6.2) aur conductivity control karne se uniform film thickness (DFT) milati hai.

175°C - 185°C par 20 minute hold time se complete cross-linking aur corrosion resistance milta hai.

Paint Defects, Causes & Remedies

Standard Industrial defect identification manual powered by Magare Industries.

Surface Defect

Dust / Foreign Particle

Particles projecting through the paint surface due to dirty environment or improper IPA wiping.

Cause: Clogged air filter, un-stripped jigs, environmental dust.
Remedy: Clean booth filters weekly, use lint-free cloth, balance booth air pressure.
Flow Defect

Orange Peel

Uneven surface texture similar to the skin of an orange caused by poor atomization.

Cause: Low gun pressure, high paint viscosity, incorrect flash-off time.
Remedy: Set air pressure to standard, maintain solvent viscosity, check gun distance (6-8").
Viscosity Defect

Paint Sagging / Run Down

Downward flow of wet paint forming heavy wavy curtains on vertical surfaces.

Cause: Excessive paint flow, too thin paint, spray gun too close.
Remedy: Reduce gun fluid output, maintain proper paint viscosity, ensure proper flash-off.

Operational Excellence Pillars

World-Class Manufacturing Principles followed at Magare Industries.

5S Methodology

  • 1S (Sort / Seiri): Unwanted items ko shopfloor se remove karein.
  • 2S (Set in Order / Seiton): Har item ke liye designated fixed place.
  • 3S (Shine / Seiso): Equipment & paint booth ki deep daily cleaning.
  • 4S (Standardize / Seiketsu): Standard Operating Procedures (SOP/WI).
  • 5S (Sustain / Shitsuke): Self-discipline aur continuous audits.

Kaizen (Continuous Improvement)

Chote-chote continuous improvements se cost reduction, safety enhancement aur rejection rate minimize karna.

PDCA Cycle Plan ➔ Do ➔ Check ➔ Act framework for root cause problem solving.
Poka-Yoke (Mistake Proofing) Paint jigs aur auto-sensors me error-proof mechanisms lagana.

TPM (Total Productive Maintenance)

Zero Breakdowns, Zero Defects, aur Zero Accidents target achieve karna.

  • JH (Autonomous Maintenance): Operators inspect, clean & lubricate spray guns & pumps daily.
  • PM (Planned Maintenance): Scheduled filter changing & AHU servicing.
  • OEE Improvement: Overall Equipment Effectiveness > 85% maintain karna.
