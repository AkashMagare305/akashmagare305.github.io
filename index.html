✨ 5S Methodology Guide

Workplace Org

5S System Ke 5 Pillars

  • 1S (Seiri): Sort - Zaroori aur gair-zaroori saman alag karein.
  • 2S (Seiton): Set In Order - Har cheez ki jagah tay karein.
  • 3S (Seiso): Shine - Workarea ki roz safai karein.
  • 4S (Seiketsu): Standardize - Rules ko follow karein.
  • 5S (Shitsuke): Sustain - Discipline banaye rakhein.
Visual Management

5S Implementation Benefits

Workplace ko neat and clean rakhne se productivity badhti hai, samay ki bachat hoti hai aur accident hone ke chances kam ho jate hain.

🔥 Industrial Safety Guidelines

PPE Gear

Personal Protective Equipment

  • Safety Helmet: Head protection ke liye.
  • Safety Shoes: Heavy objects aur chemical se protection.
  • Safety Goggles: Spark aur dust se aankho ki suraksha.
  • Ear Plugs: High noise area me kaan ki suraksha.
Fire Safety

Fire Extinguisher & Hazard Signs

Emergency exit route ko hamesha saaf rakhein. Fire extinguisher ka sahi upyog (PASS rule) aur chemical safety labels ko samajhna zaroori hai.

🎨 Paint Shop Safety & Defects

Defects Guide

Common Paint Defects

  • Orange Peel: Surface santre ke chilke jaisi textured hona.
  • Runs & Sagging: Paint ka ziada thickness ki wajah se behna.
  • Pinholes / Blisters: Paint surface par chhote bubbles ya hole banana.
  • Dust Contamination: Unclean booth ki wajah se dust aana.
Paint Booth Safety

Shop Floor Precautions

Paint booth me mask/respirator ka use karein. Chemical storage area me ventilation hona aur static grounding hona zaroori hai.

⚡ EV Scooter & Components

Main Parts

EV Key Components

  • Lithium-ion Battery Pack: Powerhouse of EV.
  • BLDC Hub Motor: Wheel ko Drive dene wali motor.
  • Motor Controller (MCU): Speed aur power distribute karta hai.
  • BMS: Battery ki safety aur charging monitoring system.
High Voltage Safety

EV Assembly Safety

High voltage wiring (Orange cables) par kaam karte waqt insulated gloves aur insulated tools ka use karein.
