DEFECT GALLERY

Paint Defects — Tap to Learn

Har defect card ke andar appearance, probable process areas, investigation approach, prevention points aur inspection checklist milegi.

Orange Peel

Uneven/textured surface appearance.

Sag / Run

Paint film flows downward and creates a visible run.

Crater / Fish-eye

Localized circular separation/depression appearance.

Dust / Nib

Foreign particle or raised surface contamination.

Pinholes

Small point-like openings/imperfections in the coating.

Adhesion / Peeling

Coating bond issue requiring controlled investigation.
