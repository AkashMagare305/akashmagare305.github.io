ACED Defects

Illustrative visual + common possible causes. Final limits must follow your plant SOP, TDS and SDS.

Thin / Low Film

Possible causes: deposition/process parameter variation, electrical contact, bath condition or part orientation.

Check: process parameter, material, equipment, cleanliness, film thickness and inspection data.

Pinholes

Possible causes: gas/air, contamination, substrate condition or process disturbance.

Check: process parameter, material, equipment, cleanliness, film thickness and inspection data.

Craters / Fish-eyes

Possible causes: oil, silicone, grease or other contamination.

Check: process parameter, material, equipment, cleanliness, film thickness and inspection data.

Poor Adhesion / Peeling

Possible causes: poor pretreatment, contamination, curing or surface condition.

Check: process parameter, material, equipment, cleanliness, film thickness and inspection data.

Sag / Run

Possible causes: local high film, drainage/orientation or process variation.

Check: process parameter, material, equipment, cleanliness, film thickness and inspection data.

Dirt / Roughness

Possible causes: bath contamination, poor filtration, rinse or environmental dirt.

Check: process parameter, material, equipment, cleanliness, film thickness and inspection data.
