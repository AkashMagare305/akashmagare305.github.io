EV Electrical Safety Education

Lithium-ion battery packs and EV electrical systems can present serious electrical, thermal and fire hazards. This website provides educational theory. Battery opening, live testing, electrical repair and high-voltage work must only be performed by appropriately trained and authorized professionals using approved procedures and equipment. Never experiment on an energized EV system.
